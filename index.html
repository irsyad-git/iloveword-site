Frequently asked questions

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?
No. Every tool runs on your device. Files never leave your iPhone, iPad or Mac unless you share them yourself.

Which file types are supported?
Word documents in .docx format, PDF, JPG/PNG images, TXT and Markdown. The old .doc format is not supported; save it as .docx first.

How do I save a result?
Tap Save / Share after a tool finishes, then choose Save to Files, AirDrop, Mail or any other app. Recent results are also kept in the History tab.

How do I delete pages from a document?
Use Split Word › Remove pages or Remove Pages, then type the pages (for example 10-11) or tap them in the page grid.

How do I change the language?
Tap the globe button at the top right of the home screen, or go to Settings › Language.
