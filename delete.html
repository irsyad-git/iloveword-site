Data deletion (iLoveWord)

Last updated: 7 November 2026

iLoveWord has no accounts and keeps your documents and History only on your device: delete them in the app (History › Clear) or by uninstalling the app.

The only data we receive is what you send through the feedback form (name, email address, message and optional diagnostics). To have it deleted, email irsyad.mukhtar@yahoo.com with the subject “iLoveWord data deletion” from the address you used. We delete all of your messages and contact details within 30 days and keep nothing else.

See also the full Privacy Policy.
